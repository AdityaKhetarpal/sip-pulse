Three answers on inflows and SIPs

  1. Equity inflows dip after a fall, maybe by chance

    A median −13.9% the month after, against +3.0% in other months. With 7 falls, the gap’s 90% range still crosses zero.

  2. SIP money dips about 1% after a fall

    A median −1.0% against +1.6%: about ₹310 crore a month. The whole 90% range sits below zero.

  3. Inflows rose in the fall month itself

    Up in 6 of 7 fall months, a median +21.7%. Found after the locked test, and its range includes zero, so it is a lead, not a finding.

Questions about the data

Which months count as Nifty falls?

A month the Nifty 50 closed 5% or more below the previous month-end. 7 months since April 2019 qualify.

What is an SIP?

A systematic investment plan: a fixed amount invested every month, set up once and paid automatically.

Where do the inflow figures come from?

AMFI’s monthly reports and SIP data, read into DuckDB, with Nifty 50 month-end closes from Yahoo Finance. 741 checks ran; 0 failed.

Which inflow tests were fixed in advance?

The two tests on the month after a fall, written down before any download. The fall-month finding came later.
